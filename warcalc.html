Solver

Solving for the defenders. The attackers above are treated as fixed.
One tick is one exchange. Allow more than one and whatever survives on both sides fights again next tick, so the solver may send a force that cannot wipe the enemy in one go and whittles them down instead — usually far cheaper up front, at the price of more troops burned and more ticks of upkeep. Every extra tick is another exchange to work out, so the search takes longer.
Max stacks is how many different loadouts the answer may mix. While there are few enough of them every combination gets tried; once there are too many to enumerate, the search instead grows the best forces one stack at a time and stops when another stack no longer pays for itself. Either way it keeps running at full speed while you are in another tab — the page title says when a search has finished.
Alicorns have no published hire price, so they cost nothing and the answer collapses to “field a handful of alicorns”. Put a price in the box above to make them compete honestly, or leave them switched off. Defenders only.
